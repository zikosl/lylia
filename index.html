A LILY FOR LYLIA

Dearest Lylia,

Since the very first sight, something about you quietly captured my heart.
I truly wished for a chance with you—just to see where our story could have gone.
I know in my heart that it won’t work out between us, and I accept that gently...
But I couldn't let the moment pass without leaving you this blooming lily—so you’d know how special you were to me from day one.
✦ Always wishing you the brightest sky ✦
